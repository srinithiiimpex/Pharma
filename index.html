Our Story

Pioneering Healthcare
Through Innovation

Founded in 1999, Boga Pharmaceuticals has grown from a single formulation unit into a multi-facility enterprise serving patients across 12 countries. Our commitment to quality and affordability drives every decision we make.

Our WHO-GMP certified facilities produce over 500 formulations across 25 therapeutic segments — each batch rigorously tested to meet international pharmacopoeial standards.

WHO-GMP & ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturing
Dedicated R&D center with 40+ formulation scientists
Fully automated quality control laboratories
Export-ready packaging in compliance with ICH guidelines
Robust pharmacovigilance and post-market surveillance
